RH FREEMINT HUNTER
==================

1. Read RH-Freemint-Hunter-Manual.pdf first. It is short.

2. Double-click RH-Freemint-Hunter.exe

   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC".
   That is normal for a new program.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

3. Your browser opens the dashboard. There is no console
   window - the program runs quietly in the background.

4. It starts in PRACTICE MODE. It cannot spend any money
   until you add a wallet and turn practice mode off.
   Watch it for a while first.

5. To close it, press "Quit app" on the dashboard.
   Closing the browser tab does NOT stop it.


WHAT THE FILES ARE
------------------
RH-Freemint-Hunter.exe        The program. Nothing to install.
RH-Freemint-Hunter-Manual.pdf The manual.

It creates these next to itself when you use it:
  .env             your settings and wallet key - NEVER SHARE
  data\mints.json  what it has minted
  hunter.log       activity log, safe to share for help


IMPORTANT
---------
Never send anyone your .env file.
Never post a screenshot of the Settings page.
